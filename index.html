Privacy Policy

Ride Angel  ·  Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Ride Angel collects, why it is collected, and what you can do about it. Please read it carefully, particularly the section on location data.

Who is responsible for your data

Milne Tech Ltd (company number 17459388) is the data controller for this app. That means we decide what information is collected and how it is used.

You can contact us about anything in this policy at support@rideangel.co.uk.

What the app does

Ride Angel is designed to help keep riders safe, particularly horse riders and anyone who rides out alone. When you start a ride, the app records your location, watches for a possible fall using your phone's motion sensors, and can alert the emergency contacts you have chosen if it detects that you may have fallen and you do not cancel the alert.

Please note: Ride Angel is an assistive tool, not a guaranteed safety device. Fall detection depends on your phone's sensors and its position on your body, and alerts depend on a working mobile data connection. It should not be relied upon as your only means of getting help.

Location data

Location is the most sensitive information this app handles, so it is worth setting out clearly.

  • Location is only recorded while a ride is active. It is not recorded when you are not riding.
  • The app asks for permission to access your location in the background. This is necessary because fall detection must keep working when your phone is locked or in your pocket. Without this permission, the app cannot protect you once the screen turns off.
  • Your route is stored against your ride history so you can look back at it.
  • Your live location is visible to emergency contacts you have added and who have accepted, but only while a ride is active.
  • If a fall is detected and you do not cancel it, your location is sent to your emergency contacts so they can find you.

You can stop location being collected at any time by ending your ride, or by changing the app's permissions in your phone's settings. Turning off location permission will stop fall detection and location sharing from working.

Why we are allowed to use your information

Under UK data protection law we must have a lawful basis for using your personal information. Ours are:

  • To perform our contract with you. We need your account details, location and contact information to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Your consent. Location tracking, including in the background, and push notifications only happen after you have given permission on your device. You can withdraw that permission at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Our legitimate interests. Keeping accounts secure and the service working properly.

Who your information is shared with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

Your emergency contacts

When you add someone as an emergency contact and they accept, they can see your live location while a ride is active, and they will receive your name and location if a fall is detected. Only add people you are happy to share this with. You can remove a contact at any time.

Where your information is held

Our database is hosted in the European Union. Our servers and some of our providers may process information in the United States, and where information is transferred outside the UK we rely on the safeguards those providers have in place, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and ride history: kept until you delete your account.
  • Emergency contacts: kept until you remove them or delete your account.
  • Sign-in records: kept for a short period and then removed automatically.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen in the app. This permanently removes:

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your ride history, including all recorded routes
  • Any fall alerts recorded against your rides
  • Your emergency contacts
  • Any entries where other riders had added you as their emergency contact

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If other riders have added you as their emergency contact, they will no longer be able to reach you through the app.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Ask us to restrict how we use it
  • Object to us using it
  • Ask for your information in a portable format
  • Withdraw your consent to location tracking and notifications at any time

To exercise any of these, email support@rideangel.co.uk. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

This app is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian is happy for you to use it and understands that the app records your location and shares it with the contacts you add.

Keeping your information secure

Information is sent between the app and our servers over an encrypted connection. Passwords are stored scrambled and cannot be read by us. Access to the database is restricted. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy
